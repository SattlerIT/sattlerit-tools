Scope

This privacy policy applies to the tool overview at sattlerit.github.io/sattlerit-tools, to the project pages of the S-IT tools at sattlerit.github.io, and to downloads of the programs via GitHub.

🌐 Hosting of the pages

Hosting by GitHub

The pages are provided via the GitHub Pages service. The provider is GitHub B.V., Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands, or GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When a page is accessed, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular the IP address, the date and time of access, the page accessed and information about the browser and operating system. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are stored for security purposes. We have no access to this data.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of the pages and programs.

Data may be transferred to the USA in this process. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). Details can be found in GitHub's privacy statement.

Downloads

The program files are provided via GitHub. When downloading, GitHub processes the data mentioned above in the same way.

🍪 No cookies, no statistics, no third-party content

These pages do not set cookies and do not use statistics or analytics services. No content from other providers is embedded, i.e. no third-party fonts, no embedded videos and no social media buttons. Images and videos are delivered from the same pages via GitHub Pages.

✉ Contact

If you contact us by email or phone, we process your details (for example name, email address and the content of your message) in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry relates to a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for handling the enquiry, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

🔗 Links to other websites

The pages contain links to other providers, for example to download portals, press articles and PayPal. These links are plain references. Data is only transferred when you click a link; the privacy policy of the respective provider then applies.

Donations via PayPal

If you donate via the PayPal link, we receive the payment details from PayPal, in particular name, email address and amount. We use them only to process and record the donation and retain them within the statutory retention periods. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR.

  • object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR).
